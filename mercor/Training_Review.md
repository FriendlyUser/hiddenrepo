### **Module Overview: Professional Writing Failure Modes**

This training module is designed to help users identify common **"AI-slop" style failure modes** in professional writing.

* **Core Focus:** Evaluating writing across four contextual signals.
* **Key Principle:** A pattern should be flagged only when it is **conspicuous, repeated, unearned, or harmful** to the requested writing—rather than based on an isolated phrase or punctuation mark.

---

### **Training Sections / Structure**

1. **Overview** *(Current section)* – Introduction to identifying AI-style writing failure modes.
2. **Formulaic** – Identifying overly rigid, predictable, or repetitive structure.
3. **Lack of Substance** – Spotting superficial, vague, or empty phrasing.
4. **Wordy / Indirect** – Catching unnecessarily verbose, roundabout, or indirect communication.
5. **Unnecessary Framing** – Flagging superfluous introductions, meta-commentary, or setup language.
6. **Industry Standards** – Contextualizing writing expectations against professional benchmarks.
7. **Practice** – Practical exercises to test and refine identification skills.
